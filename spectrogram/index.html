ℹ How to compare

Load a file (WAV, MP3, or FRF: TRF/TRV/AvC/AvR/CSV/MAT) or record from the mic into Sample A and Sample B below — only one mic recording can run at a time.

Switch to 🔎 Single to view just one sample, full width.

Switch to ⛰ Difference to see Sample A's intensity minus Sample B's, as one plot — 3D Surface makes the peaks and dips easiest to read. Mirror instead shows A and B side by side around a center line, without subtracting them.

Switch to 🎙 Live to watch the microphone continuously while you dial in FFT settings — nothing is saved to Sample A or B.
